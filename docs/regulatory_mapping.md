# Regulatory mapping

Each rule of the Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks - Asset Classification, Provisioning and
Income Recognition) Directions, 2026 ("ACPIR"; `docs/reference/RBI-Directions.pdf`) and of the
Directions it refers to, with where the engine implements it and the test that checks it with a
hand-built example. RBI values live in `config/rbi_2026.yaml` (with the paragraph),
`config/rbi_iracp_2025.yaml` and `config/rbi_credit_facilities.yaml`; developer choices are marked
`rbi_specified: false`. Paths are relative to `src/ecl/` and `tests/`.
